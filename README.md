# Gabbers

Gabbers is an invoicing app for freelancers, contractors and small studios. It exists because sending a client a professional invoice shouldn't mean wrestling with Word templates or paying for heavy accounting software.

You add your details, your client and a few line items, and Gabbers does the rest. It works out the subtotal, discount and VAT as you type, shows a live preview of the invoice, and exports a clean PDF you can send right away. It's built with Philippine freelancers in mind, so the peso and 12% VAT are the defaults, and you can also bill in USD, EUR or GBP. You can add your Wise details to the invoice so clients know how to pay you.

Accounts use email and password sign-in with email verification, so each person's saved invoices stay in their own account.

Gabbers is a portfolio project, so the pricing plans on the landing page are for demonstration only and nothing is charged.
